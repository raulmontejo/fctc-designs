About the coop

Technology should serve the community, not the other way around.

We're a small, worker-owned cooperative. We believe everyone deserves access to the power of technology — and the freedom to use it on their own terms.

That shapes how we work: open tools, plain language, and no surprises. When a project ends, you own what we built together.
