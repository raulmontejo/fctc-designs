06 Signature Element
PCB Branch Decoration
Extracted branch fragments from the logo SVG, used at low opacity as environmental texture along page edges, section dividers, or hero backgrounds. The traces and pads echo the circuit-tree motif without competing with content.

Technology rooted in community

Branch SVGs from the logo, rendered at low opacity as ambient texture. Works on dark or light backgrounds.

--pcb-trace: var(--neutral-900);   /* trace lines */
--pcb-pad: var(--white);          /* pad fill */
--pcb-pad-ring: var(--neutral-600); /* pad outline */
--pcb-substrate: var(--green-400);  /* board color */
Notes
Design Rationale

Why green-400 as the anchor — The logo green (#96ca51) is naturally bright and sits at the lighter end of a typical scale. Placing it at 400 gives us usable darker values (500–700) for hover states and text that pass WCAG AA contrast on white backgrounds.

Why warm neutrals — The logo's dark fills (#3a3b3a, #231f20) have a slight warm cast. The neutral scale matches that warmth rather than going blue-gray, reinforcing the approachable, community-first feel.

Why amber — A golden earth tone complements the yellow-green primary without clashing. It gives the palette grounding and warmth beyond what green alone conveys — think growing things, sunlight, soil.

Dark mode — The CSS tokens file includes a prefers-color-scheme: dark override that swaps semantic tokens while keeping primitives intact. Ready to use if the site needs it.
